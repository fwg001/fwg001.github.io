    When to stop

    Stop any exercise if symptoms increase by more than 2 points on a 0–10 scale during or after activity. Symptoms may include: headache, dizziness, nausea, fatigue, neck pain, or visual strain.

    Tips for success

    Keep your chin tucked and neck long in all exercises. Move slowly and with control — no jerky motions. Focus on quality over quantity, combined with good posture and regular movement.
